Nepali Bikram Sambat Calendar
Nepal · Official State Calendar · BS 2000–2082
The official calendar of Nepal. Each month's length is set by astronomical observation — there is no fixed formula or pattern. It runs 56–57 years ahead of Gregorian. 1 Baisakh (mid-April) marks the Nepali New Year. Conversion anchor: 1 Baisakh 2052 BS = 14 April 1995 AD.
